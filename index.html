Our Vision

Born between two universities, Batch & Beam is more than a café — it's a design-forward refuge for focus, connection, and comfort. Crafted by an architect and an engineer, this space blends aesthetic and intention, bringing you hand-made desserts inspired by structure, and coffee brewed with purpose.

We believe your environment matters. We believe your coffee should spark something. And we believe a quiet space, a warm espresso, and the right atmosphere can change your day.
